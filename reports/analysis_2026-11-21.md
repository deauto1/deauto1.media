# Performance analysis, Nov 14–20 (CMO)

## Instagram
- Pattern is all-or-nothing: most reels get about 110–200 views. 13 reels broke out at 1,300–2,000.
- What the breakouts have in common:
  - None are brand films.
  - All have a skip rate (viewers gone in the first 3 seconds) of 42% or less.
- Breakout formats:
  - X vs Y (⚔️): «قير عادي ولا أوتوماتيك» 2,038, «تيربو ولا سحب طبيعي», «الدبّة ولا الشكمان».
  - «اختار كورتك/قيرك» race.
  - «الفني قال»: الرديتر 1,687, الكمبروسر, البواجي.
  - «صاحبك يقول»: نيتروجين 1,587, سخّن.
  - Parts drama: المساحات 1,577.
  - «جاوب قبل 3»: الشكمان 1,392.
- Brand films: 104–144 views each.
  - Skip rate: Arabic 42–58%, English 78–87%.
- English reels on Instagram: 110–158 views, skip rate often 50–80%. No breakout yet.

## TikTok
- Median views per video:
  - Nov 15–17: 454–483.
  - Nov 18–20: 122–151.
- The drop starts on Nov 18 and hits every format, including formats that used to win. That points to account-level distribution.
- Likely causes, unproven: posting cap (12/day), repetitive templated look, low watch time on brand films. ~95%+ of views come from For You.
- Exceptions after Nov 18:
  - Children's Day «مكانه وين؟» 2,675 views, 3 shares.
  - «صوت الشكمان» 722.
  - «0-100» 384.

## Decisions
1. Brand message rides inside winning formats: hook, then content, then a brand signature at the end. Pure brand films drop to 1–2 per day, redesigned hook-first. Never open with the logo or «وش هو».
2. Target skip rate under 40%: a question or conflict written on screen in frame 1, and the voice starts within 0.3 s.
3. Repeat winners with new angles: ⚔️ / race / «الفني قال» / «صاحبك يقول» / «جاوب قبل 3» / safety.
4. English: 1 per day, test TikTok only. Pause English on Instagram until skip rate improves.
5. TikTok test: 6 strong videos per day for 3 days instead of 12. If the median recovers above 300, keep it.
6. Daily loop at 11:00: pull yesterday's numbers, keep or kill each format, produce the next batch, and keep the schedule ≥3 days ahead.
7. Sales are still 0 despite ~30k views per week. Conversion fixes from the audit need the owner's approval.
