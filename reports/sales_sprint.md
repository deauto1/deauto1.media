# Sales sprint — first 10 paid orders (CMO, 2026-11-23)

## The diagnosis, in numbers (Nov 1–23)
- Instagram: ~360 reels, ~70,000 reel views, **~19 comments in total**. People watch, nobody engages.
- Store: 159 visits → 10 add-to-cart → 7 orders, **all free items. Paid: 0 SAR.**
- No affiliates, no active coupon, no landing page, no reviews on any part, Part 1 page has 1 image and no inside-page preview.
- Store social links: Instagram + TikTok only. Snapchat (reaches ~89% of Saudi adults per DataReportal 2026) and X are missing.
- Creator list (20 accounts) and B2B offer were drafted on Nov 7 and never sent — they waited for approval and I didn't push.

**Root cause:** we made views, not buyers. The videos never show the product, never give a reason to buy now, and the path from video to checkout is "link in bio → store → find the right part among 30 products".

## The sprint (7 days)
1. **One product, one path.** Every post sells Part 1 only. On-screen at the end: the product cover, what's inside, the price, and one action.
2. **Comment → DM with the direct link (Instagram).** ManyChat free plan (Instagram DM, up to 1,000 contacts). Keyword: «جزء1». DM below.
3. **TikTok Business account** → clickable website link in bio with no follower minimum. We already use no music, so the business-account music limit costs us nothing.
4. **Product-reveal videos** («وش داخل الجزء 1»): real pages of the PDF on a phone, 3 lessons you'll learn, price, CTA. Fewer posts, each one with a sales job: ~6/day instead of ~16.
5. **Borrow audiences, pay only on sales:** Salla affiliate opportunity (commission per sale, tracked link valid 1 year, payouts on the 14th and 28th). Offer the 20 creators: free copy + commission.
6. **B2B:** driving schools, car clubs, technical institutes — one deal = many copies.
7. **Proof:** first buyers get asked for a store review; reviews go into the next videos.
