
## 2026-11-28 · Ref 2: "pause challenge" (wantsandneeds founder, 30k followers, sold-out drop)
- Hook: «Only 1% can pause at the right time» plus a red outline. The viewer tries to stop the video when the person fits the outline.
- Missing by a few frames shows «so close», so people rewatch. Average watch time ends up longer than the video, and IG pushes it.
- The correct frame hides a reward: «new sweatsuit live · DM us the screenshot for a discount». That got 29k DMs and a sales spike.
- Lesson: take a format that is already viral and add a conversion layer that turns views into sales.
